HymnPilot privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

HymnPilot helps you learn to sing your part of a song.

What stays on your phone

Sheet music, slides, photos and recordings you add are read and stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded. Your lesson progress is stored only on your phone.

Microphone

When you turn on the mic, HymnPilot listens while you sing to tell you if a note is high or low. The sound is analyzed live on your phone. It is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

YouTube

If you add a YouTube link to a song, the video plays from YouTube inside the app, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that video.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

HymnPilot does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not share data with anyone.
