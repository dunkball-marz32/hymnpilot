What do the colors mean?

Green means on pitch: within 25 cents of the note. Yellow means a little high or low: more than 25 but less than 75 cents off. Red means 75 cents or farther off. Positive (+) is high; negative (−) is low. One semitone is 100 cents. No voice detected means Listening, with no grade.
